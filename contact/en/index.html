Contact

To report a bug, send us your thoughts or ask about something published here, please use the form below.

We read everything that arrives and use it when deciding what to build and fix next. Please note, though, that we do not reply to messages individually. Since no reply is coming, there is no need to include your contact details.

When reporting a bug, it helps enormously if you say which game and which screen it happened on. There are fields in the form for your device and browser.

Form

The form is written in Japanese, but please feel free to write your message in English — we read both.

If the form does not appear, a browser extension or setting may be blocking the embed. In that case, please try another browser.

For how we handle what you send, please see our privacy policy.
